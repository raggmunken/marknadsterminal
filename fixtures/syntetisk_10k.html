Nordvik Test Components Inc.
Annual report for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025

Item 1. Business

Nordvik Test Components Inc. (“we”, “our” or the “Company”) designs and manufactures sensor modules for vehicles and for industrial equipment. We operate two segments, Automotive and Industrial.

Customers

We sell our products to vehicle manufacturers, industrial distributors and retailers. In fiscal 2025, Haldenfjord Motors Corp. accounted for approximately 21% of our net sales. The loss of this customer would have a material adverse effect on our results.

Sales to Brightwater Retail Group represented 12.5 percent of the Company’s consolidated revenues in 2025. Customer C accounted for 10% of our net sales. No other customer accounted for 10% or more of our net sales.

In our Industrial segment we also sell a large part of our sensors through Tallgrass Distribution LLC, which made up roughly one fifth of that segment’s revenue and which we regard as a significant customer.

Suppliers and raw materials

We buy components from a number of suppliers. Kestrelmark Semiconductor Ltd. is our sole source for the signal controller used in most of our modules. Any interruption at this supplier could delay shipments to our customers.

Aluminium housings are supplied by Polarvik Castings Ltd under a three year agreement. We believe other sources are available at comparable cost.

Competition

The markets in which we operate are competitive. We compete on product performance, reliability and price with a number of larger companies that have greater resources than we do.

Employees

As of December 31, 2025 we had 1,840 employees, of whom 1,210 worked in manufacturing. None of our employees is covered by a collective bargaining agreement.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to risks and uncertainties. The risks described below are not the only ones we face. Forward looking statements in this report involve known and unknown risks.

Our revenue depends on a small number of customers and a concentration of sales in the automotive market. A downturn in vehicle production would reduce demand for our products.
